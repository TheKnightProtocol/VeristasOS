VeristasOS analyzes digital interactions across content, URLs, language, privacy, and threat patterns to provide explainable risk assessments and actionable guidance.

🛡️ SCAM SHIELD: Rule-assisted Scam Radar protecting against KYC fraud, OTP theft, and bank impersonation.
🔐 PRIVACY SHIELD: India-First sensitive data masking for Aadhaar, PAN, UPI IDs, and OTP PINs.
📡 THREAT INTELLIGENCE: Multi-signal threat aggregation connecting Scam DNA attack fingerprints.
🎓 SAFETY ACADEMY: Digital trust guidelines and safety awareness tips for online safety.
