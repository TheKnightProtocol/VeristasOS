⚙️ How VeristasOS Works

VeristasOS evaluates submitted content across a multi-stage verification pipeline to produce an explainable risk assessment:

1
Input Ingestion: Accepts unstructured news text, claims, and image media uploads.
2
Stylometric & Text Core: Analyzes sensational vocabulary, exclamation density, and ALL-CAPS frequency using NLTK heuristics.
3
Media Forensics: Computes SHA-256 hashes, perceptual 64-bit dHash, EXIF tags, and extracts OCR text overlay.
4
Source Provenance: Evaluates domain legitimacy, publisher attribution, and publication metadata.
5
Evidence Aggregation: Cross-references extracted claims against authoritative indexed records.
6
Explainability Output: Displays highlighted trigger words and structured signal breakdowns.
